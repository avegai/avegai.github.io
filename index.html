03 — about

Ten-plus years turning client briefs into interactive audio and haptic experiences. I work alongside creative teams, research where an experience actually breaks down for users, and turn those findings into both better sound design and the strategy around a release; a client advisor rather than an executor of briefs.

Currently designing UX/UI sound and haptic feedback at amp Sound Branding in Munich, running the nu.wav label, and studying for a Sound Art masters at HMTM.
