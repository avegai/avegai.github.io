case study / UX sound

Calm UI sounds for an investment app

A UX/UI sound system built from the brand's Sonic DNA and tuned with psychoacoustic analysis, so the interface stays calm at the moments people are most anxious about their money.

This is a personal case study, made to show my capabilities. It's inspired by my UX sound work, but it isn't the exact client project.

    the problem

    People open an investment app at tense moments: after a market drop, before a large order, when an alert arrives. Most interface sounds are built to grab attention, bright and sharp and urgent. Here the brief was the opposite. Every sound had to help people stay calm.

    built from the brand

    None of the sounds are stock. Each one is derived from the brand's Sonic DNA track, its motif, timbre and harmony, so the app sounds like the same brand as everything else it puts into the world.

    measuring calm

    Taste isn't enough when t

    Taste isn't enough when the goal is how a sound makes someone feel. In my work I analyse candidate sounds in Python for sharpness and roughness, two qualities strongly linked to sounds feeling harsh or stressful, and use the results to guide revisions and explain choices with data rather than opinion.

    what I learned

    From client work: testing early beats defending taste later. Putting sounds and measurements in front of people early gives them something concrete to react to, and shortens sign-off.
